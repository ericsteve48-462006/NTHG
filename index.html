everyday apps, essentially

The essentials, and nothing you didn't ask for.

Everyday apps are supposed to disappear into your routine — the calculator you reach for without thinking, the calendar you glance at once a morning. Somewhere along the way, apps like these picked up features nobody asked for: accounts, subscriptions, notifications about notifications. NTHG goes back to the actual problem — you needed small tools, not small platforms — and ships exactly that as Essential+. No bloat, no upsell, no account wall. Just the essentials, doing the essential, unglamorous work they were built for.

1. The essentials

By downloading Essential+, you agree to receive four (4) small apps that do roughly what they say and nothing else. No account is required. No account is offered. There is nothing to sync, because there is nothing worth syncing.

2. Honesty clause

Some numbers displayed by these apps are not accurate. Some times are invented. At least one alarm will never sound. This is disclosed here, once, in a box you are currently scrolling past.

Bruh. You're at least twenty-something, you went to school, you can read just fine — and you're still not reading this. We know. We wrote it anyway, and you're still scrolling, so: respect, honestly.

3. No warranty, expressed or implied

NTHG is provided "as is," which is legal language for "we're not bringing back the flashlight." Use of the Calculator for actual math is discouraged. Use of the Calendar to remember anything important is discouraged. Use of Click Me. Please. for personal growth is, unfortunately, also discouraged.

4. Data

We don't collect your data. We don't have a server. This whole thing runs in your browser and forgets everything the moment you close the tab, much like you will forget most of this paragraph.
